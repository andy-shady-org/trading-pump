## Read-out (added 2026-11-03)

**Both lanes NO-GO.** Judged with `trading-core-audit verdict` and each
lane's pre-registered file, unchanged (rule hashes `620ae5f7…` and
`6561daf7…`, as registered).

**How the run went.**
- Started 2026-10-30 18:58:56 UTC; the engine stopped at 2026-11-02
  19:49:10 UTC (`run_stopped`), 50 minutes after the planned end, because
  `--max-cycles 259200` counts cycles, and cycles ran longer than 1 s.
- **Buying was off for 27 of the 72 hours.** The daily 10% loss lock
  tripped at 2026-11-01 13:23:21 UTC (balance 1.6186 of a day's start of
  1.8065 SOL) and held buying for 24 h. Buying resumed at 13:23 on 2 Nov,
  and the cumulative 25% lock tripped at 15:40:47 UTC (1.4996 of 2.0000
  SOL), which pauses buying until the operator resumes it. Each lock sold
  the open positions (`emergency_liquidation`, 5 positions in all). Monday's
  US session after 15:40 UTC was not traded.
