## Close-out – 2026-10-17 13:14 (T + 1 h 33 min)

The run was stopped by the operator at 13:14:29. The daily circuit breaker had tripped at 12:41:20 (T + 60 min) and blocks buying until the 00:00 UTC reset (03:00 local), so the remaining ~21 h of that day, and every later day with the same results, would produce no data. ### Analysis
1. **The bot was blind while holding (BUG-112, High).** PumpPortal refuses `subscribeTokenTrade` without an API key funded with at least 0.02 SOL; it answers with a `message` that the bot silently drops. Confirmed after the run: 3 subscriptions, 0 trade messages, 3 refusals. Consequences:
   - exits only see the price when the 5 s health loop reads the curve, so the 10% stop-loss filled at −12.5% to −14.7% gross;
   - the order-flow and momentum engines got no trades at all;
   - the capture holds no market trades (each bought mint has only its create, buy and sell records), so replay cannot evaluate exits and `--compare-audit` cannot be meaningful.
   The v0.2.0 run had the same blind spot ("a third of positions never received a price update").
2. **The entries had no edge.** 17 of 34 positions exited at exactly −5.4% / −5.5% gross: the curve never moved after the buy, so nobody else traded the token in 45 s. At 13:20 every one of the 34 mints was back at the launch market cap (~28.0 SOL), and none had graduated. Holding longer would not have helped; the stagnant timeout cut losses rather than causing them.
3. **The cost floor is 6.4% per round trip.** A flat exit costs about 2% Pump.fun fees, 3% paper entry slippage (300 bps) and ~0.4% rounding, plus 1% fixed costs (two 50,000-lamport tips and network fees). A token has to rise more than ~6.5% just to break even, and only 2 of 34 did.
4. **The breaker worked as designed.** 10% of the 0.25 SOL paper equity is 0.025 SOL, about 37 flat round trips; the bot reached it in an hour (about 23 buys an hour). With the same entries, every day of a 72 h run would be about 1 h of trading and 23 h locked.
5. **Plumbing is healthy.** Memory flat, no exceptions, no drops, every entry gate fired, holder data present for 98% of screened tokens, and alerts were delivered.
